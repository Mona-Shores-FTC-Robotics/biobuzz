One Autonomous that checks the robot during INIT, picks its own plan, and shows why on one Driver Station screen. With a healthy robot the drive team reads one green line and presses PLAY.

Why this plan

    The partner

    The one choice a person makes before every match, from scouting: what the partner does. It starts on the other start from ours, so where we are placed says where it is. A toggles Launch & Park and Just Park, each always in its own colour, here, on the Driver Station and on the scouting sheet; there is no default, so until it is picked the screen says NOT READY and the light flashes white.

    We startLaunch & ParkJust Park
    RightR-QualsR-Quals: TIP 1 is ours at once anyway
    LeftL-Quals: waits for the partner's TIP 1, makes it itself by 9.2 s if it never comesNot a plan: a Just Park partner gives no TIP 1, so we start on the right. NOT READY

    The camera says which start we're on. With no camera, ◀ ▶ say it. A partner-specific Auto (one built for a scouted team) is a new entry in the list.

    The decision, in order

    Pinpoint not ready → PARK ONLY camera ON and a start confirmed → SMART (unless Y forced BACKUP) otherwise → BACKUP partner, alliance or side unknown → NOT READY until a button picks it the route → from our start and the partner turret not healthy → held forward; every plan already aims by turning

    One pure function with no hardware in it, unit-tested at a laptop with a test for every line above. The screen and the match log print the same reasons.

    Lock

    A drive team can wait 1 to 10 minutes at the field holding gamepads, so the screen locks. In the mock above, press a few buttons and then leave it for 30 seconds.

    One chord

    Hold both bumpers for 2 seconds to lock or unlock. Hard to do by accident, and no setting uses a bumper.

    Locks itself

    Once the plan is READY and nothing has been pressed for 30 seconds. The first line counts down, so it's never a surprise.

    Buttons do nothing

    Locked, every override is ignored, X and B included. A press flashes "LOCKED: hold LB + RB 2 s to change" so nobody wonders.

    The plan is frozen

    Checks keep updating. One that changes turns amber, "changed since lock", and the first line adds CHECK. A person unlocks and decides.

    Pairing is safe

    The Driver Station pairs gamepads with Start + A and Start + B, and B is red alliance. Every press while Start is held is ignored.

    Everything is logged

    Each override, lock and unlock goes into the match log with its time, so a wrong pick can be traced afterwards.

    The robot light (later)

    The screen only helps the person holding the Driver Station. A light on the robot lets a partner, the field staff or a coach in the stands catch what the drive team missed. It shows the alliance, because anyone can check that against the field without knowing our code, and a wrong alliance is the costliest mistake. How it lights says whether all is well, so red never has to mean "error".

    LightMeans
    Solid red or blueReady, on that alliance
    Red or blue, flashingThat alliance, but not ready, or something changed since the lock
    Red and blue, alternatingMismatch: the camera sees one alliance and the buttons chose the other. The buttons still win; the screen says CHECK
    White, flashingNo alliance, partner or side picked yet

    With the camera off there is nothing to disagree with a wrong button, and the solid colour is the only safeguard: a blue glow on the red side. Limits from the manual: lighting faster than 5 Hz invites scrutiny, so nothing here passes 2 Hz, and a powered light can't be the alliance sign, so it sits beside the required sign.

    What INIT checks

    INIT may hold motors and servos still (R103.B) and the robot must be motionless once INIT is done (G304.H), so every check reads and nothing moves.

    Disabled means fully off: no start check or alliance proposal, no TIP detection, and no tag fixes into Pedro's filter. A camera that is wrong but trusted drags the pose, and every path after it.

    At PLAY and after

    The plan locks

    At PLAY the plan is frozen and written to the match log as one event with its reasons, so a bad match can be explained from the .wpilog.

    It stays on screen

    The Match page keeps "SMART · RIGHT · RED" as its first line through AUTO, above the route's own lines.

    Camera lost mid-match

    Costs no more than a backup: every SMART wait has a time limit, and its timeout branch carries on sensibly. That is a rule for every SMART route.

    Insurance

    Emergency Park L and Emergency Park R sit at the bottom of the DS list: no checks, no camera, no screen. If Smart Auto's own code fails in INIT, they still park.
