# Design meeting, 8 Oct 2026

The full-robot CAD (branch `claude/robotics-meeting-notes-lq2y55`) was presented as a complete design for the team to
weigh, with the deck and the 3D viewer. Notes from the student who presented it.

## Decided

- **Turret on a servo**, with the two-encoder gear trick for absolute position (`doc/motors-and-servos.md`).
- **Two launcher motors.**
- **The feeder is independent of the launcher**: the flywheels have to reach speed without firing.
- **The transfer is gravity-fed or tied to the intake.** Which one is still being worked out.

## In the current robot design

- Two free-spinning wheels under the intake help pull pieces in.
- The intake is short, with no vector wheels for now.
- A float, derived from the one in this CAD.
- A corner upright wheel, to help at the garden corner.

## Still open

- Whether any more of this CAD is adopted (electronics bay, wiring plan, the wider FLOWER bar, the lane).
- The turret hood.
- More CAD updates are expected; each one gets diffed against this CAD so the team sees what changed and what it
  costs or gains.
