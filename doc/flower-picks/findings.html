What fails when a pick does not get 4

  • The push stops short. All three picks that ended 4.0 in or more short got 0, with the FLOWER still full (Belding Q-4 both picks, Fruitport Q-4 20245's SCORING pick). Of the 41 picks that ended less than 4 in short, 2 got 0.
  • The stick is lifted (19429). The FLOWER keeps 1 to 3 pieces (Belding Q-13, Q-26 and Q-32 WALL picks; Fruitport Q-2 WALL pick took 0 and left about 3). On every limp pick the servo's pulse was cut during the push: 0.8 to 0.9 s into it on a first pick, and right at contact on a second pick, because the stick went down earlier on the drive in. This is the failure #699 fixed on 20245.
  • A piece drops at the intake. One ball lands on the floor beside the robot while the FLOWER empties (Fruitport M-3 and M-10, Belding Q-19 and Q-1). This happened on 20245 even with the stick held.
  • Taken but not fired. On some 19429 picks fewer balls left in the volley than went in, or the light stayed green after it (Fruitport Q-2, Q-28, Q-39, M-6). That is a feed problem, and it hides a good pick in any count taken from the volley.
