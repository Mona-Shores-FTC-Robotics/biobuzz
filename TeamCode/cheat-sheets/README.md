# Cheat-sheet logos

The printable controller sheets are generated by `CheatSheetTest` from `controls/ControlScheme`
into `TeamCode/build/cheat-sheets/` (`./gradlew testDebugUnitTest`, then open and print landscape).

To put a team's logo in the sheet header, add it here as `logo-19429.png` (Infrared) or
`logo-20245.png` (Ultraviolet); an `.svg` works too. A square image on a transparent or white
background prints best. Without one, the sheet draws the team number on the team's colours.
