# Ideas for the "what to work on" sheet (from the Belding review)

- **Lane-empty signal.** A beam break at the front lane slot (next to the feeder) tells the driver "empty, go collect" (light bar / rumble), ends an auto-drive volley when the lane is empty instead of a fixed 3.5 s feed (a full volley is out in ~2.2 s, so ~1.3 s per volley feeds an empty lane), and may count shots better than the launcher dip. Sent to the "Counting launches accurately" session for a recommendation.
- **Shot counting is unreliable** (launcher dip misses shots): counts, pieces per volley and "4th piece lost" are floors / unreliable until fixed.
- **NECTAR may be slow through the lane** (~0.4 s more per piece), but this came from counted shots, which miss pieces: unconfirmed until the shot counter is fixed or video shows it.
- **First piece is slow out of the gate:** ~0.8 s vs ~0.47 s for each later piece in volleys where all 4 were counted. The 'over 1 s' tail seen earlier is in miscounted volleys only, so it is probably a counting artifact. Gate card saved in gate-card-saved.html to restore once counting is fixed.
- **Pinpoint dropouts:** the code keeps refusing after the Pinpoint recovers (fix in progress in its own session).
- **Auto-drive cancels if a stick is already moving** (#495).
- **Ultraviolet drives back slower when full** (23 vs 29 in/s heading out); Infrared doesn't. Driving or pieces? Timed run, empty vs 4 pieces.
- **Pieces hesitate at the lane entrance, mostly on Ultraviolet.** Time under the entrance colour sensor for pieces passing through: median 0.29 s (UV) / 0.42 s (IR), but 24% of UV's pieces took over 1.0 s against 11% of IR's, whatever the lane count. In 28 cases a UV piece was waiting right behind a hesitating one. Every piece over 1.0 s trips "full" for a moment: 34 quick false fulls at Belding (29 UV, 5 IR). Next: watch the match video at the listed moments (entrance, sensor bracket, or hand-off into the lane?). The fix is likely mechanical; the 1.0 s full hold should not get longer, since the "go" signal must stay fast.
- **Faster, more accurate "full" and "empty".** Slot-3 beam break: full = entrance colour sensor AND slot-3 beam occupied ~0.15 s (green ~0.85 s sooner, no false fulls from slow pieces). Slot-1 beam break at the feeder: end the auto-drive feed once the beam has been clear ~0.4 s (~1 s saved per auto-drive volley; UV ran 46), count launches directly, stage the first piece at the gate. Both on digital ports (bulk read). Supersedes the lane-empty line above. Issue to open after the video.
- **FLOWER pick counts:** the entrance sensor undercounts FLOWER picks (pieces arrive nose to tail); reconciled with launches it is 3.2 / 2.0 per Auto pick vs 2.4 / 1.2 sensed. The lane-full signal is not reliable during a pick (it can read full while the stick holds a piece under the sensor).
