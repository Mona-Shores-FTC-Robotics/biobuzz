TAKE 2c: a flat wheel with the servo BESIDE the arm, so the FLOWER stick's arms stay straight (27 Nov 2026)
==========================================================================================================
Checked against the 26 Nov robot STEP through the stick's whole 0-162 deg swing, with a straight arm:
mount shelf 5.6 mm, servo 7.3 mm, servo ears 7.6 mm, flat wheel 8.6 mm. With the corner one goBILDA hole off in
any direction (up, back or forward) it still clears by 3.2 mm or more. Stowed: inside the 18 in cube.

PRINT (PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 40 % gyroid, no supports; times for a Prusa MK3/MK4-class)
  2c_corner_mount_left.stl     PRINT 1. Shelf down on the bed, as the file is. 10 g, about 55 m.
  2c_corner_mount_right.stl    PRINT 1. Its mirror. Same settings.
  2c_straight_arm_optional.stl OPTIONAL, PRINT 2. Only if you want printed arms (keyed tip, so the shaft and the
                               mountain can't turn). Today's aluminium channels work as they are. Lay it flat as
                               the file is, turned 30 deg to fit a 250 x 210 bed. 66 g, about 4 h 26 m each.

THE ONE CHANGE TO THE STICK
  Move the LEFT arm 15 mm (0.6 in) further out, so it sits like the right arm (|Y| 6.60 to 7.08 in from the
  middle). In the robot CAD it sits 15 mm further in, right over where the left servo goes. A 15 mm spacer at its
  pivot and a 15 mm spacer (or shaft collar) at its tip on the REX shaft; the shaft is long enough.

PER CORNER
  - the printed mount: its upright leg flat on the outside of the grey 5-hole intake channel, 6 x M4 x 12 mm
    screws + 6 M4 nylock nuts through the channel's two front hole columns (the circled one and the one behind
    it), nuts inside the channel
  - today's goBILDA Speed servo, standing on the mount's shelf, spline DOWN, its body through the shelf's window,
    its ears held by 4 x M4 x 10 mm screws (they thread into the 3.4 mm printed holes)
  - one 2.5 in intake wheel with a 1/2 in hex bore, about 1/2 in thick, lying flat under the shelf, on your usual
    servo-to-1/2 in hex hub, with a screw and washer under it. Its middle is at the POLLEN's middle (1.4 in up).
  - the wheel now pushes POLLEN sideways into the intake: check its spin direction on the bench
